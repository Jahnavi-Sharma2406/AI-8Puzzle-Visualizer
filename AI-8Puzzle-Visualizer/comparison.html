How the algorithms differ

Uninformed search

BFS, DFS, DLS, and IDDFS search without estimating how close a state is to the goal.

Informed search

GBFS and A* use heuristic information to guide exploration toward promising states.

Shortest solutions

BFS and IDDFS find shortest solutions under equal move costs. A* can also do so with suitable heuristics and implementation.

*Standard assumptions apply. Completeness and optimality depend on graph handling, costs, and heuristic properties.
